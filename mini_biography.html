Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He was widely known as the "Missile Man of India" and inspired millions of young people.

Early Life

Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He came from a humble family and worked hard to achieve his dreams of becoming a scientist.

Important Achievement

One of his biggest achievements was helping India develop its space and missile programmes. His contribution to India's missile and space technology earned him great respect. He believed in the power of dreams and hard work and encouraged young people to work towards their goals. His dedication to the nation made him an inspiration to many.

He served as the President of India from 2002 to 2007.

Words That Inspire

He often reminded young people that dreams are not what you see in sleep, but something you work towards.

You have to dream before your dreams can come true.
